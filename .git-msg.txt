feat(ui): inviting reports whether it actually went out

The dialog's pick filled the composer and closed itself — so it looked successful whether or not an
invitation was ever sent, and the sender had to remember to press Enter in a conversation afterwards. That is
the "发成功还是未成功" requirement, unmet.

NOW

    sending               list disabled, "正在发送…"
    sent, recipient up    「已邀请 李四，对方在线，会立即收到」
    sent, recipient away  「已邀请 李四，对方当前离线，上线后会看到」
    failed                「发送失败：<code>」

The three-way outcome is deliberate. "Sent" and "will be seen now" are different promises, and collapsing them
into one tick is how a sender concludes the feature is broken when nobody replies immediately.

WHY THE SEND LIVES IN THE DIALOG

The alternative was forwarding an `onInvite` prop the parent exists only to pass along. Keeping it here lets
the dialog own its own states, and the parent supplies facts rather than plumbing.

WHY THE LIST DISABLES WHILE SENDING

Each invitation mints a single-use link. A double click would mint two live ones, and only one would ever be
redeemed — with no way to tell which.

THE HOST ENDPOINT REUSES `person/send` SEMANTICS

Two code paths that mint invitations would eventually disagree about `maxUses` or expiry, and the disagreement
would be visible only to whoever received the wrong link.

FALLBACK KEPT

Without the channel, picking a person still fills the composer with the instruction. A host without Connection
loses the feedback, not the ability to invite.

Verified: 427 tests, and on a live host the endpoint answers
`{"ok":true,"value":{"dmId":"dm-67f0737867b1","online":false}}` with the invitee's inbox then holding one
`group-invite` carrying a working link.
