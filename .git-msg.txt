feat(ui): an unbound conversation gets a real pane, and a button that always opens it

THE DEAD END THIS FIXES

The button and the pane were gated on the same condition — "does this conversation have a group" — so
hiding the button when nothing was bound left no way to reopen the pane after closing it. A control
that opens a panel has to exist whenever the panel can be opened; what changes is the label.

    bound    群成员 3
    unbound  协作群        (no number — "群成员 0" would claim the group is empty)

THE PANE NOW ANSWERS THREE QUESTIONS

The old body was one sentence and no action, which is the kind of pane a reader learns to ignore. A
new conversation actually has three questions, and the pane answers them in order:

  1. what is a collaboration group   — so the feature is nameable
  2. how do I get into one           — a pasted link, or the left sidebar's list
  3. here is the link I was given    — a paste box, then one press

The paste box fills the composer rather than calling the tool: this component renders before any
Session exists and holds no member credential, so the Session turn is the only writer. The button is
dimmed rather than hidden while the box is empty, because a disappearing button teaches nothing.

A FOOTNOTE FOR THE CONFUSING CASE

Somebody who HAS joined a group from another conversation will otherwise find this pane insisting they
have not. It says so plainly: that group belongs to that conversation, join it here too and both see
the same messages.

Also fixed while here: the button's tooltip read 展开「undefined」的群成员 when nothing was bound.

Verified: 422 tests, `node --check` clean, and the live page shows the 协作群 button with no count plus
a pane containing both routes and the paste box.
