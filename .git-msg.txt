feat: edit my personal headline, distinct from my role in one group

Two facts, two places, and until now only one had a form:

    「我在这个群负责什么」  member.roleAbout, changes per group
    「我是谁」              person.headline, follows me into every group and onto every card

Somebody who is 负责对账 in one project and 兼产品 in another needs both; one field would force a choice.

The editor now shows them as two labelled fields rather than one field with a hint, because a user who edits
the wrong one gets a change that does not appear where they expected — and the difference is invisible in a
screenshot. The label states the scope: 「个人介绍（所有群共用，显示在同事名片上）」 vs 「群内介绍（只在这个群生效）」.

WHY A NEW SKILL WAS NEEDED

`group/role` writes `about` and `tags` — facts about ONE membership. The headline lives on the person row, so
a member of a remotely hosted group had no way to set it: the skill for it did not exist. `person/profile` now
does, and takes a group id only because a credential belongs to a membership — the store then writes the
person that credential identifies. There is no path that names somebody else.

TWO DETAILS THAT KEEP IT HONEST

- an untouched headline is omitted from the payload rather than sent empty, because an absent field means
  "leave it alone" and a cleared one would wipe the value on every role edit
- the editor reads the current headline from the person table, not the roster row: the headline is a person
  fact, and a roster cached before it existed would not carry it

OBSERVED, NOT INTRODUCED

`text` comes back empty for BOTH `group/role` and `person/profile` when called through the gateway; `meta`
carries the values. Pre-existing, unrelated to this change, and recorded here so the next person does not
spend a turn on it.

Verified: 427 tests, and on a live host `persons` gains
`张工 headline='后端工程师，专注对账系统'` while `groups` keeps `roleAbout='这个群的负责人'`.
