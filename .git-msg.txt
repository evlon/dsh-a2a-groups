feat(ui): inviting happens in a dialog, not in the member list

Listing colleagues inline fails in two ways that only appear at scale, and the second one is worse:

  - a team of five gets a friendly little row; a directory of a thousand gets a wall that pushes the
    roster off the pane
  - the first twelve were shown with no indication that the rest existed, so a reader with a thousand
    colleagues would reasonably conclude most of them were not invitable

A dialog separates the two questions. "Who is in this group" stays in the pane; "who could join it"
happens in a surface sized for choosing. The pane keeps one quiet button.

SEARCH IS THE PRIMARY CONTROL, NOT PAGINATION

Nobody pages through a thousand colleagues; they type two characters of a name. The list under the box
is what an empty search shows — capped at six, with the count of what is not shown stated explicitly,
because a silently truncated list is how somebody decides a person is not invitable.

Matching covers name, headline and tags, so "the DBA" finds 赵六 even without recalling the name.

TWO MISTAKES WORTH RECORDING

1. The dialog was first wrapped in a `display: contents` div so it could sit outside the pane. Four
   layout tests failed immediately — and correctly: `MembersPane()` returning an element whose
   `props.style` IS the pane's style is the contract those tests describe. The fix was to notice that
   `position: fixed` escapes the pane's clipping without any wrapper at all.

2. Replacing `membersLayout` wholesale lost the original `root`/`header` definitions, which the same
   tests caught. Inserting next to the anchor instead of over it is the rule.

Verified: 422 tests, and a live run — open the pane, press 邀请同事入群, the dialog shows the search box
and 赵六, and picking him closes the dialog and leaves a ready instruction in the composer naming both
`personId` and `groupId`.
