fix(security): the panel no longer carries message bodies

`__DSH_A2A_GROUPS__` is injected into the index page's HTML, and it included the last eight feed
entries WITH their text. Two consequences, both bad:

  1. **Content left the process.** Into HTML, which a proxy may cache, a screenshot may capture, and a
     browser extension may read. A member is entitled to read their group; that is not the same as the
     body being written into every page render.
  2. **It crossed conversations.** The snapshot is global while groups are bound per Session, so one
     conversation's messages appeared in another conversation's page. The binding only means something
     if the data respects it.

WHAT THE CARD SHOWS NOW

    李四 发来一条消息
    3 人 · 2 条未读 · 1 分钟前

Which answers "should I open this?" — recency, unread count, and who is talking. The words are in the
conversation, behind a deliberate `a2a_group_read` with a member credential.

FOUR SHAPES, ONE RULE

The panel accepts four summary formats because the snapshot has passed through several generations:
`lastFromName`/`lastTime`, `lastPost`, `lastMessage`, and a raw `feed` tail. All four contribute an
author and a time; **none contributes text**. Dropping the body at every door matters because a
compatibility path that forwards it is the same leak in a place nobody audits — and it is how the
current bug would have survived the first fix.

ONE RULE WORTH KEEPING

A timestamp alone is not a message. `lastPostAt` with no author means something happened, not that
somebody spoke, and rendering 「有新消息」 for it invents a line the group never had. An author or a
system marker is required; ordering reads `lastTime` separately, so nothing is lost.

Verified: 422 tests. Four card tests were rewritten rather than repaired — they asserted that message
text appears on the card, which was the behaviour being removed. They now assert that the summary names
the author and that the body is absent.
